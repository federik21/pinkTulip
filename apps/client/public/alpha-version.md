# BRW Battles Alpha

This is an early version of the game, built for testing and feedback.

- Play a live browser battle with friends or AI opponents
- Draft schools and mages, then build your own spell setup
- Move through the lodge, trigger room effects, and fight for control
- Save a session and reconnect to continue a battle later

## What is included

The prototype already lets you create a room, invite up to three other mages, and play a full match from the lobby through battle, preparation, and cleanup. You can also choose a solo game against basic AI and try a local sandbox setup.

## What to expect

This is an unfinished fan-made prototype, not a final version of the game. Rules, card text, visual are still evolving as we test the system and smooth out rough edges.

## Notably missing from game engine

- Events, Quests, Mage and their Personal spells cards and Quests are placeholders, but actual rules are implemented.
- Dark side of Spell cards.
- Actual card and component images.

## Things to keep in mind

- Some features are experimental and may change
- Room resets and session changes can happen while the prototype is being refined
- The experience is meant for playtesting and feedback, not a polished launch build
